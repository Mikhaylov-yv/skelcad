# LLM/agent-based CAD generation, verification and design reasoning: papers, benchmarks, datasets (2023 – Oct 2026)

Scope note: notes compiled 2026-10-12 from arXiv abstract/HTML pages, project pages and GitHub READMEs. Web-search budget was exhausted near the end, so a few items (LLM4CAD JCISE numbers, CADialogue full text, CADMate full text, Autodesk/industry details beyond Zoo and Bernini) rest on abstracts/snippets only; these are flagged in Gaps. All numbers are quoted with the benchmark they came from; numbers from different papers/protocols are NOT directly comparable (point sampling, normalization, CD scaling ×10³ vs raw, "median" vs "mean" differ).

## Catalog: what each paper/benchmark/dataset does (task, representation, metrics, best results, failure analysis, authors' lessons)

### Takeaway
The field moved in three waves: (1) 2021–2024 sequence models over DeepCAD-style sketch-extrude command sequences (DeepCAD, Text2CAD, CAD-MLLM); (2) 2024–2026 LLM/VLM code generation in CadQuery (CAD-Recode, cadrille, CADEvolve, CAD-Coder, CADSmith) with execution + geometric reward/RL; (3) 2026 "engineering-validity" benchmarks (BenchCAD, CADEngBench, CAD-bench, Text2CAD-Bench, CADBench, CADENA-Bench) and FEA/assembly agents, which show that executable, plausible-looking CAD still fails most functional/engineering checks.

### Cited Findings

**Foundational datasets (non-LLM, still the substrate of nearly every benchmark)**
- DeepCAD (Wu, Xiao, Zheng, ICCV 2021): 178,238 CAD designs as sketch-extrude command sequences, 90/5/5 split, built from Onshape models in ABC — [arXiv 2105.09492](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09492)
- Fusion 360 Gallery (Willis et al., Autodesk, 2021): reconstruction subset of 8,625 human design sequences using only sketch+extrude; "Fusion 360 Gym" exposes construction as an MDP; code at github.com/AutodeskAILab/Fusion360GalleryDataset — [arXiv 2010.02392](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02392)
- SketchGraphs (Seff, Ovadia, Zhou, Adams, 2020): 15 million sketches from real CAD models, each a geometric constraint graph (primitives as nodes, designer constraints as edges); tasks: sketch generation, constraint prediction — [arXiv 2007.08506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.08506)
- AutoMate (Jones, Hildreth, Chen, Baran, Kim, Schulz; SIGGRAPH Asia 2021 / TOG 40(6)): scraped 255,213 Onshape assemblies with 3,989,164 mates; after cleaning 92,529 unique top-level assemblies and 541,635 unique mates; mate types FASTENED 62.1%, REVOLUTE 12.7%, PLANAR 11.8%; SB-GCN on B-Reps; in-CAD mate suggestion tool reached 72.2% accuracy; parts as Parasolid+STEP, metadata as parquet (Dryad release 2023) — [arXiv 2105.12238](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.12238), [project](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/projects/automate), [Dryad](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.2547d7wvw)
- JoinABLe (Willis et al., CVPR 2022): joint-axis prediction on B-rep graphs reaches 79.53% accuracy vs ~80% human baseline; joint pose via neurally guided search minimizing overlap volume/contact-area cost; released Fusion 360 Gallery Assembly-Joint dataset (non-commercial license) — [CVPR 2022 paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Willis_JoinABLe_Learning_Bottom-Up_Assembly_of_Parametric_CAD_Joints_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf), [supplemental](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/supplemental/Willis_JoinABLe_Learning_Bottom-Up_CVPR_2022_supplemental.pdf)
- Mates2Motion (2022): 65% mate-type accuracy on its full dataset (per search snippet) — [arXiv 2208.01779](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.01779)

**Text-to-CAD, sequence representation**
- Text2CAD (Khan et al., DFKI / RPTU Kaiserslautern-Landau / MindGarage / BITS Pilani; NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight): ~170K DeepCAD models with ~660K text prompts at 4 abstraction levels (L0 abstract … L3 expert), generated with Mistral + LLaVA-NeXT; BERT encoder + 8-block autoregressive decoder over 256-class tokenized CAD sequence. L3 results vs DeepCAD baseline: Line F1 81.13 vs 76.78, Arc F1 36.03 vs 20.04, Circle F1 74.25 vs 65.14, Extrusion F1 93.31 vs 88.72, median CD (×10³) 0.37 vs 32.82, mean CD 26.41 vs 97.93, IR 0.93% vs 10.00%; GPT-4V preference for Text2CAD 63.24% at L3; user study with 5 CAD designers — [arXiv 2409.17106](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.17106), [project](https://sadilkhan.github.io/text2cad-project/)
- CADFusion (Wang, Yuan, Sun, Bian; ICML 2025): LLM backbone alternating "sequential learning" on ground-truth parametric sequences and "visual feedback" stage rewarding sequences that render into preferred objects; authors' lesson: both signals are critical and alternating them balances learning — [arXiv 2501.19054](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19054)
- CAD-MLLM (Xu et al., 2024): Omni-CAD dataset ~450K instances with text, multi-view images, point clouds and command sequences; adds topology/surface-enclosure metrics beyond CD — [arXiv 2411.04954](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04954), [project](https://cad-mllm.github.io/)
- CAD-Llama (Li et al., 2025): converts command sequences into "Structured Parametric CAD Code" (SPCC) with hierarchical semantic descriptions; adaptive pretraining + instruction tuning; claims to outperform autoregressive and LLM baselines (no numbers on abstract page) — [arXiv 2505.04481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04481)
- Pointer-CAD (Qi et al., CVPR 2026): LLM emits command sequence where face/edge-selecting operations (fillet, chamfer) use a pointer into the B-rep built so far; ~575K-model dataset; authors' lesson: plain command sequences cannot do entity selection and discretizing continuous variables causes topological errors — [arXiv 2603.04337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04337)
- CADFS (Pyatov et al., CVPR 2026): ~450K real-world CAD models as executable FeatureScript (Onshape language) programs covering 15 operations; fine-tunes a large VLM for text-to-CAD and image reconstruction; ablations credit FeatureScript representation, extended op set, and representation-aligned text — [arXiv 2605.01925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01925), [project](https://voyleg.github.io/cadfs/)

**Reverse engineering / reconstruction as CadQuery code**
- CAD-Recode (Rukhovich et al., SnT Univ. Luxembourg + Artec3D, 2024/25): point cloud → CadQuery Python via Qwen2-1.5B + point projector, trained on 1M procedurally generated sequences. DeepCAD: mean CD 0.30, median CD 0.16 (×10³), IoU 92.0%, IR 0.4% vs prior SOTA CAD-SIGNet 3.43 / 0.28 / 77.6% / 0.9%; Fusion360: 0.35 / 0.15 / 87.8% / 0.5%; real-world CC3D: 0.76 / 0.31 / 74.2% / 0.3% vs CAD-SIGNet 14.82 / 2.90 / 42.6% / 2.5%. Stated limitation: no revolve/fillet expressiveness; procedural data contains no human-designed models — [arXiv 2412.14042](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.14042)
- cadrille (Kolodiazhnyi … Rukhovich; Lomonosov MSU / ETH Zurich / Innopolis; ICLR 2026 Oral): Qwen2-VL-2B taking point clouds, multi-view images or text → Python code; SFT then online RL (Dr. CPPO = Dr. GRPO + CPPO) with reward 10·IoU and −10 for invalid code, hard-example mining. Point cloud DeepCAD RL: median CD 0.17, IoU 90.2, IR 0.0 (CAD-Recode 0.18 / 87.1 / 3.1); image DeepCAD RL: 0.17 / 92.2 / 0.0; CC3D image RL: 0.57 / 65.0 / 0.1; text (DeepCAD/Text2CAD) SFT: median CD 0.21, IoU 81.1, IR 1.4 vs Text2CAD 0.37 / 71.5 / 3.7. Lessons: RL on image data also improved point-cloud results ("surprising"); online RL beats offline DPO; naive data mixing can hurt SFT — [arXiv 2505.22914](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22914v3), [code](https://github.com/col14m/cadrille)
- CADEvolve (Elistratov et al., Lomonosov MSU / FusionBrain Lab / Innopolis, Feb 2026): AlphaEvolve-style loop from 46 hand-written CadQuery seed generators, GPT-5-mini edits children, execution + geometry + visual-text checks → 7,945 parametric generators, ~1.3M scripts (body also says 2.72M SFT scripts; inconsistent). Image2CAD, CADEvolve-C big (RL1) vs cadrille RL: DeepCAD CD 0.15 / IoU 92.6 / IR 0.2 vs 0.17 / 92.2 / 0.1; Fusion360 0.16 / 87.2 / 0.5 vs 0.17 / 84.6 / 0.1; MCB (RL2) 0.52 / 55.2 / 0.4 vs 0.87 / 47.6 / 2.5. Limitation: synthetic data may not match industrial distribution — [arXiv 2602.16317](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16317), [code](https://github.com/zhemdi/CADEvolve), [data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kulibinai/cadevolve)
- CADENA (Kabisov … Zhemchuzhnikov, Lomonosov MSU, Aug 2026): stepwise reverse engineering — Qwen2-VL policy sees multi-view render of target (green) vs current build (red) and appends one CadQuery-DSL operation at a time, keeping the prefix with highest IoU; CADENA-Bench = 3,396 mechanical parts (MCB+TMCAD+CADNet) in 6 families; new GMS metric (position+normal matching, defined for non-watertight). CADENA-RL vs cadrille / CADEvolve: DeepCAD IoU 96.1 vs 89.7 / 92.4; Fusion360 94.1 vs 84.8 / 88.1; MCB IoU 88.2 (CD 0.22, IR 0.7) vs 66.2 / 72.9; CADENA-Bench GMS 67.0 vs CAD-Recode 48.9, cadrille 49.8, CADEvolve 52.9. Lesson: stepwise gains grow with geometric complexity; failure modes: countable features (gear teeth) approximated, occluded geometry invisible, early frame errors not undone — [arXiv 2608.00799](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00799), [code](https://github.com/zhemdi/cadena), [bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kulibinai/cadena-bench)
- CADFather (Savrasov … Zhemchuzhnikov, Lomonosov MSU, 6 Oct 2026): training-free agent (Qwen3.8-27B assistant) coordinating CADENA-RL proposals, algorithmic fitting proposals (cross-section extrusions/revolves) and signed-distance parameter optimization. DeepCAD IoU 0.987 / GMS 0.983 / IR 0 (cadrille 0.897, CADEvolve 0.924); Fusion360 0.976; MCB 0.913 (cadrille 0.662); CADENA-Bench IoU 0.909, GMS 0.721; CADBench IoU 0.930 (CADFit 0.859); BenchCAD voxel IoU 0.968 at ~$0.046/part vs vendor-reported Claude Opus 5.5 with tools 0.962 at ~$5.94/part and GPT-6 Astra 0.959 at ~$1.75/part (different subsets; authors flag as contextual). Ablation: removing algorithmic proposals on MCB raises IR 0→0.12. Single parts only — [arXiv 2610.09127](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09127), [code](https://github.com/kulibinai/CADFather)
- CADBench (Doris, Sony, Nehme, Syla, Heyrani Nobari, Ahmed; MIT; May 2026): 18,000 evaluation samples in 6 families from DeepCAD, Fusion 360, ABC, MCB, Objaverse; 5 input modalities (clean mesh, noisy mesh, single-view, photorealistic, multi-view render); 11 systems, >1.4M generated programs; metrics: volumetric IoU, surface IoU, CD, Valid Shape Rate, token count, operation count. Clean inputs: best specialized mesh-to-CAD CADFit IoU 0.859 / VSR 0.981; CADEvolve 0.707, cadrille 0.687, CAD-Recode 0.673; best general VLM Claude Opus 4.7 IoU 0.412 / VSR 0.798, GPT-5.4 IoU 0.158; CAD-Coder (image) IoU 0.354 / VSR 0.950. Noisy mesh IoU drop: CADFit −0.051, cadrille −0.108, CAD-Recode −0.162, CADEvolve −0.577. Three failure modes: degradation with face count, brittleness of specialized models under modality shift, metric-dependent rankings — [arXiv 2605.10873](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10873), [code](https://github.com/anniedoris/CADBench)
- Zero-to-CAD (Ataei, Askari, Malekshan, Jayaraman — Autodesk-affiliated authors per DesignQA coauthorship; Apr 2026): LLM in a feedback-driven CAD environment synthesizes ~1M executable parametric sequences beyond sketch-extrude without real construction-history data; curated 100K release; fine-tuned VLM claimed to beat GPT-5.2 on multi-view image→CAD (no numbers on abstract page) — [arXiv 2604.24479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24479)
- UniCAD (Chen et al., Jun 2026): unified multi-modal multi-task benchmark (point→CAD, text/image→CAD, CAD QA) and UniCAD-MLLM; claims SOTA on UniCAD and Fusion360 (no numbers on abstract page) — [arXiv 2606.05058](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05058)

**Text/image-to-CadQuery code with LLMs/VLMs (single part)**
- Text-to-CadQuery (Xie, Ju, May 2025): adds 170K CadQuery annotations to Text2CAD; fine-tunes 6 open LLMs; best top-1 exact match 69.3% (from 58.8%), CD −48.6%; argument: generate CadQuery directly to remove intermediate representations and exploit LLM Python ability — [arXiv 2505.06507](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06507), [code](https://github.com/Text-to-CadQuery/Text-to-CadQuery)
- CAD-Coder (Guan … Yu, Beihang / HKU; 2025): Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 110K text–CadQuery–3D triplets built with DeepSeek-V3 + CD filtering; SFT on 8K high-quality, CoT cold start 1.5K, GRPO on 150K with CD-based geometric reward + format reward. Text2CAD test: mean CD 6.54, median CD 0.17, IR 1.45% vs Text2CAD 29.29 / 0.37 / 3.75%, GPT-4o 133.52 / 45.91 / 93.00%, Claude-3.7-sonnet 186.53 / 134.16 / 47.03%. Lessons: 8K high-quality > 70K medium (6.54 vs 9.89); CD-only reward produced invalid code after ~200 RL steps — auxiliary rewards matter — [arXiv 2505.19713](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19713v3)
- CAD-Coder (Doris, Alam, Heyrani Nobari, Ahmed; MIT; 2025, ASME JMD 10.1115/1.4071305): open VLM fine-tuned on GenCAD-Code (>163K image–CadQuery pairs); 100% valid syntax; outperforms GPT-4.5 and Qwen2.5-VL-72B on image→code (IoU 0.675 per secondary source); some generalization to real photos and unseen operations — [arXiv 2505.14646](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.14646), [code](https://github.com/anniedoris/CAD-Coder), [ASME](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4071305)
- FutureCAD / "Towards High-Fidelity CAD Generation via LLM-Driven Program Generation and Text-Based B-Rep Primitive Grounding" (Li et al., Mar 2026; listed as ICML 2026 by a search result): LLM writes CadQuery and specifies selections in natural language; BRepGround transformer maps text queries to B-rep faces/edges; SFT then RL; new real-world dataset — [arXiv 2603.11831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11831), [code](https://github.com/JohanStackk/FutureCAD)
- LLM4CAD (Li, Sun, Sha; UT Austin; JCISE 2025): GPT-4 / GPT-4V zero-shot CAD program generation from text, text+image, text+sketch on synthetic mechanical components (gears, springs…) with MTurk descriptions; metrics Cap1 = syntactically correct program, Cap2 = IoU; finding: multimodal inputs matter more for complex geometries (springs, gears) — [NSF PAR record](https://par.nsf.gov/biblio/10661832) (full text unreachable at time of writing)
- LLM4CAD-Editor (Sun, Sha; Jun 2026): DSL with feature-name entity selection for functional/operation/parameter-level edits; 35,139 instruction-program pairs; 32B fine-tuned model; parsing accuracy 96.3% (parameter), intent satisfaction 82% (functional), IoU 0.935 / 0.871 / 0.708 (parameter / operation / functional); claims 1.4× editing robustness over Python-script approaches — [arXiv 2606.20607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20607)
- Query2CAD (Badagabettu, Yarlagadda, Barati Farimani; CMU; 2024): LLM generator + refiner producing executable CAD macros with BLIP2 visual feedback and optional human feedback; GPT-4 Turbo first-attempt success 53.6%, refinement +23.1%, most gain from the first refinement iteration — [arXiv 2406.00144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00144), [code](https://github.com/akshay140601/Query2CAD)
- CADSmith (Barkley, Loghmani, Barati Farimani; Mar 2026): multi-agent CadQuery pipeline, inner loop fixes execution errors, outer loop uses OpenCASCADE programmatic checks (bounding box, volume, solid validity) plus independent VLM judge; RAG over CadQuery docs. 100-prompt custom benchmark (3 tiers): execution 95%→100%, median F1 0.9707→0.9846, median IoU 0.8085→0.9629, mean CD 28.37→0.74 vs zero-shot; claim: visual feedback alone "cannot resolve dimensional errors" — [arXiv 2603.26512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26512)
- BlenderLLM (Du et al., CUHK-Shenzhen; Dec 2024): bpy (Blender Python) scripts; BlendNet ≈8K samples (2K human-verified, 6K GPT-4o-validated, from 50K generated); CADBench (their own, distinct from MIT CADBench) = 700 instructions (500 synthetic, 200 real forum); GPT-4o judge. BlenderLLM iter-3 avg score 0.748 / syntax error 3.4% (Sim) and 0.664 / 3.5% (Wild) vs o1-Preview 0.687 / 15.6%, Claude-3.5-Sonnet 0.593 / 15.6%, GPT-4o 0.565 / 21.4%. Self-improvement (generate–filter–retrain) beat epoch accumulation; LLM–human judge κ=0.791 — [arXiv 2412.14203](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.14203), [code](https://github.com/FreedomIntelligence/BlenderLLM)
- CADCodeVerify / "Generating CAD Code with VLMs for 3D Designs" (Alrashedy et al., Georgia Tech; ICLR 2025): CADPrompt benchmark = 200 NL prompts with expert CadQuery code; VLM generates and answers validation questions about rendered object then corrects. GPT-4 few-shot: IoGT 0.939→0.944, point-cloud distance 0.155→0.127, Hausdorff 0.494→0.419, compile rate 96.0%→96.5% (3D-Premise baseline: 0.942 / 0.137 / 0.446 / 91.0%); Gemini zero-shot compile 85.0→84.5%; CodeLlama few-shot compile 67.0→73.5%. Failure analysis (50 cases): structural configuration errors 48%, logical 18%; Q&A answer accuracy 64.6–68.2%; gains plateau after 2 iterations; human-in-the-loop still slightly better (PC dist 0.120 vs 0.137) — [arXiv 2410.05340](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05340v2), [code](https://github.com/Kamel773/CAD_Code_Generation)
- EvoCAD (Preintner et al., Leiden; IEEE ICTAI 2025): evolutionary optimization of sampled CAD code with VLM + reasoning LM on CADPrompt; two Euler-characteristic topological metrics; claims best topological correctness — [arXiv 2510.11631](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11631)
- Test-Time Scaling via Verifier-Free Consensus Selection (Haag, Kacan, Fuchs, Lohse; Aug 2026): sample N CAD programs, compile, return the candidate most agreeing with the pool; geometric consensus beats random selection in every LLM/prompt variant, CD −1–10%, and beats the SOTA method's own verifier on 3 geometric metrics — [arXiv 2608.09706](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09706)
- ReliCAD (Zheng et al., Sep 2026): requirements → structured design specs → constraint-aware parametric instructions executed by a CAD kernel through an "Agent-ready API", with runtime evidence and verification-feedback repair; 99.8% validity, IoU 0.8753 on HistCAD + own editing dataset — [arXiv 2609.22325](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22325)
- CADDesigner (Fan et al., 2025): general-purpose agent for conceptual CAD from text+sketch with interactive requirement elicitation, visual feedback, case knowledge base — [arXiv 2508.01031](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01031)
- CAD-Assistant (Mallis et al., SnT Luxembourg / Artec3D; 2024/25): GPT-4o planner with FreeCAD Python API, sketch/solid recognizers, constraint checker, cross-section extractor. SGPBench CAD-QA 2D 0.791 vs GPT-4o 0.686; 3D 0.857 vs 0.782; autoconstraining on ~700 SketchGraphs sketches PF1/CF1 0.979/0.484 vs Vitruvion 0.706/0.238; hand-drawn parameterization CD 0.680 vs Davinci 1.184 — [arXiv 2412.13810](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.13810v3), [code](https://github.com/dimitrismallis/CAD-Assistant)
- CAD-HLLM (ACML 2025): text-to-CAD split into Plan Generator (symbolic plan) and Parameter Completor — [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=iF9P4PiGj9); AIDL (2025): solver-aided hierarchical language for LLM-driven 2D CAD with error feedback — [arXiv 2502.09819](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.09819)
- VideoCAD (Man, Nehme, Alam, Ahmed; MIT; 2025): >41K synthetic videos of CAD UI operations (horizons up to 20× longer than prior datasets); VideoCADFormer beats behavior-cloning baselines; VQA benchmark for MLLMs — [arXiv 2505.24838](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24838)

**B-rep-native and B-rep understanding**
- BrepLLM (Deng et al., Dec 2025): UV-sampled B-rep → graph → BrepEncoder tokens aligned with CLIP text, LoRA into LLM; Brep2Text = 269,444 B-rep–text QA pairs; classification + captioning only; B-rep generation is future work — [arXiv 2512.16413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16413)
- BrepARG (Jan 2026): holistic token sequence of B-rep geometry+topology for autoregressive generation — [arXiv 2601.16771](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16771); AutoBrep (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025) — [arXiv 2512.03018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.03018); ParaCAD (Jul 2026) surface-centric tokenization — [arXiv 2607.17093](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17093)
- B-repLer (Liu, Dutt, Li, Mitra; SIGGRAPH 2026): language-guided B-rep editing in a learned latent space without construction history; BrepEDIT-240K dataset — [arXiv 2508.10201](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10201), [code](https://github.com/yilinliu77/Brepler)
- Embodied CAD (Liu, Zhou, Hao, Yang; Jun 2026): solver-grounded LLM agents over a stratified L0–L4 skill library producing parametric B-rep assemblies; grammar constraints, deterministic parameter resolution, solver-derived rewards (SFT warm-up + GRPO) — [arXiv 2606.31252](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31252)

**2026 engineering-validity benchmarks**
- BenchCAD (Zhang … H. Chen, University of Virginia; May 2026): 17,900 execution-verified CadQuery programs over 106 industrial part families (bevel gears, springs, twist drills); 4 settings: vision QA, code QA, image→code, instruction-guided code edit. Vision2Code: Gemini 3.1 Pro (thinking) IoU 0.279 / exec 79.8%; Claude Opus 4.7 IoU 0.274 / exec 94.0%; GPT-4o 0.188; o3 IoU 0.50 but exec 5.6%; CADEvolve v3 IoU 0.750 / exec 92.7%; cadrille-RL 0.068; their Qwen3-VL-2B RL-IID 0.752 / exec 98.9%. Code edit (headroom-normalized): GPT-5.3 (thinking) 0.865, Claude Opus 4.7 0.853, Gemini 3.1 Pro 0.837, GPT-4o 0.615; replacing text instruction with target render drops all models to near zero. Code QA: Gemini 3.1 Pro 0.836; Vision QA 0.587. Failures: missing fine structure (threads, chamfers), wrong workplane, replacing sweep/loft/twist-extrude with sketch-extrude, lost parametric abstraction (DIN 2095 spring); ~64% of nominally successful edits silently corrupt unrelated features. Data CC-BY-4.0 (HF BenchCAD/BenchCAD), code MIT — [arXiv 2605.10865](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10865v1), [project](https://benchcad.github.io/BenchCAD_webpage/)
- Text2CAD-Bench (Wang … Zhou; Wuhan Univ. / BitInf / Nanjing Tech / CAS; May 2026): 600 human-curated examples L1 200 / L2 200 / L3 100 / L4 100 (L4 real-world domains, single solids only); CadQuery target, compared to DeepCAD command sequences; metrics CD ×10³ (30K points), IR, voxel IoU 256³, L4 VLM judge GLM-4.6V. GPT-5.2: L1 CD 44.31 / IR 11.1% / IoU 0.59; L2 60.38 / 20.0% / 0.50; L3 93.46 / 68.0% / 0.23. Claude-4.5-Sonnet L3 CD 70.13 / IR 70%; Gemini3-Flash L3 IR 12.8% but IoU 0.20. Domain model Text2CAD: IoU 0.08 at L1. L4 IR: Gemini3-Flash 17%, GLM-4.7 50%, Claude 54%, GPT-5.2 61%, MiniMax M2.1 81%. IR rises from ~15% (L1) to 70–90% (L3) mainly from sweep/loft/shell; CadQuery beats command sequences on validity and accuracy; "executability, geometric accuracy, and design-intent understanding are largely independent" — [arXiv 2605.18430](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18430v1)
- CADEngBench (Singh, Dubey, Amador Herrera; MBZUAI; Aug 2026): P-track 300 parts (159 BenchCAD CadQuery + 141 Fusion 360 Gallery reconstruction items) × {zero-to-CAD, functional edit} = 600 tasks; A-track 150 body pairs from Fusion 360 Assembly-Joint j1.0.0; 8 models. P-track L0 program/solid validity: Claude Sonnet 4.5 58.0%, Kimi K2.5 53.3%, Gemini 3 Flash 52.7%, GPT-5.2 41.3%, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B 13.3%; L1 engineering/DFM requirements: Gemini 30.0%, Claude 28.3%, GPT-5.2 22.0%; L2-E edit: Gemini 72.3%, GPT-5.2 70.3%; L3 FEA pair pass (CalculiX linear static vs reference): Gemini 46.3%, Claude 35.8%, GPT-5.2 32.8%. 1,030/2,400 programs pass L0 but only 432 pass L1 → 58.1% of executable outputs violate ≥1 engineering/DFM requirement. Editing ≫ generation (1,071 pairs passed only editing vs 231 only generation); single independent body additions 247/248 vs joins/cuts/multi-body edits 40.2–45.8%. A-track: Entity@1 up to 62.5% (Gemini) but Typed@1 only 23.3%, end-to-end kinematic pass 15.8% (Gemini), 11.7% (GPT-5.2), 12.5% (Claude); overall only 11.5% of requests pass end-to-end; 542/960 rank-1 entity pairs correct but only 143 also right on joint family + body order (26.4%); pin-slot joints 0%. Rank correlations: generation vs editing ρ=0.214; parametric vs assembly ρ=0.048; FEA reach vs agreement ρ=0.024. Limitations: pairwise joints only, linear static FEA only — [arXiv 2608.09296](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09296)
- CAD-bench (Dhruv Saini; ICML 2026 Workshop "Agents in the Wild"): 17 public tasks in 4 tiers (easy/medium/hard/insane; tier weights 1/2/3/4) plus a private 7-task functional set; Build123d (not CadQuery); Harbor 0.19.0 + Docker harness; checks: dimensional/pose verification, reference-geometry gates, thread-profile analysis, Blender rigid-body simulation; supports one-shot code and agent harnesses producing STEP. Strongest standalone model 59.9% overall; functional tasks near zero for most standalone runs; agent harnesses better but still fail on interfaces, standards-like details, mechanisms — [ICML page](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/67851), [GitHub](https://github.com/cad-bench/cad-bench), [results page (empty table at fetch time)](https://cad-bench.github.io/)

## Key question 1: Which CAD representation works best for LLMs — code (CadQuery/build123d), command sequences, parametric JSON, B-rep tokens?

### Takeaway
The evidence through Oct 2026 favors executable Python CAD code (overwhelmingly CadQuery; build123d in CAD-bench; KCL at Zoo; FeatureScript in CADFS) over DeepCAD-style command sequences for LLMs, with the strongest direct comparison (Text2CAD-Bench) showing CadQuery beats command sequences on both validity and accuracy; command sequences and B-rep tokens remain competitive for from-scratch trained reconstruction models, and hybrid "code + B-rep grounding/pointers" (FutureCAD, Pointer-CAD, LLM4CAD-Editor DSL) is the emerging answer to the entity-selection problem (fillets, chamfers, mates).

### Cited Findings
- Text2CAD-Bench compared CadQuery against DeepCAD-style command sequences on L1–L2 and found "CadQuery code outperforms command sequences in both validity and accuracy"; under command sequences IR rises across all models — [arXiv 2605.18430](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18430v1)
- Domain-specific sequence model Text2CAD has the lowest IR on Text2CAD-Bench but near-zero IoU (0.08 at L1, 0.04 at L2–L3); CADFusion and Text2CADQuery show IR up to 90% on some levels — [arXiv 2605.18430](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18430v1)
- Text-to-CadQuery argues command sequences "must be converted into CAD representations such as CAD vectors, which requires training from scratch," whereas CadQuery "removes intermediate representations and uses LLMs' existing Python and spatial reasoning strengths"; fine-tuned open LLMs reached 69.3% top-1 exact match and −48.6% CD — [arXiv 2505.06507](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06507)
- CAD-Recode chose CadQuery because off-the-shelf LLMs can read the output, enabling editing and CAD QA from point clouds; limitation: lacks revolve/fillet expressiveness in their DSL — [arXiv 2412.14042](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.14042)
- The Melbourne/NTU survey observes "many recent papers that work with LLMs and CAD code have chosen CADQuery, as it follows a design-intended approach and is built in Python", while also noting that parametric (JSON) generation "is often preferred by researchers" because producing fully executable code is hard for LLMs and generating parameter values alleviates many challenges; it does not name a single winning format — [arXiv 2505.08137](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.08137)
- Pointer-CAD (CVPR 2026): "Command sequences can't support entity selection, which limits complex edits. Discretizing continuous variables causes topological errors"; pointer-based B-rep face/edge selection reduces both — [arXiv 2603.04337](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04337)
- FutureCAD argues parametric CadQuery and B-rep "are intertwined in feature-based CAD" and pairs LLM CadQuery generation with a BRepGround transformer that resolves natural-language selection queries to B-rep primitives — [arXiv 2603.11831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11831)
- LLM4CAD-Editor's feature-name DSL turns "fragile coordinate-based reasoning into natural language-based reasoning" and reports 1.4× editing robustness over Python-script approaches, 96.3% parameter-edit parsing accuracy — [arXiv 2606.20607](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20607)
- CADFS: ablations show the FeatureScript representation, the extended 15-operation set and representation-aligned text descriptions each help; prior generative CAD was "limited to sketch-extrude operations" — [arXiv 2605.01925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01925)
- CAD-Llama converts sequences into "Structured Parametric CAD Code" with hierarchical semantic descriptions because "LLMs lack exposure to parametric sequences during pretraining and lack direct 3D awareness" — [arXiv 2505.04481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04481)
- Zoo moved from direct text-to-B-rep generation to LLM-driven KCL code generation; "Code as the medium: Zoo found that LLMs' code-writing strength, combined with a first-class CAD language, was a more practical path than direct geometry generation"; B-rep preferred over meshes for precision/editability/manufacturing — [zoo.dev/research/zookeeper](https://zoo.dev/research/zookeeper)
- CAD-bench uses Build123D as its target language — [GitHub cad-bench](https://github.com/cad-bench/cad-bench); CADEngBench, BenchCAD, CADSmith, AssemCAD, DFM-Redesign, FEA agents all use CadQuery — [arXiv 2608.09296](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09296), [arXiv 2605.10865](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10865v1), [arXiv 2607.05123](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05123)
- No head-to-head build123d vs CadQuery LLM study found; only anecdotes (Reddit users hoping build123d "should be a lot easier for LLMs than CadQuery (unfortunately the models didn't learn it yet)") — [redlib r/openscad thread](https://redlib.hbubli.cc/r/openscad/comments/1bynbdh/generate_openscad_with_ai)
- B-rep token generation is active but separate from LLMs: BrepARG holistic token sequence — [arXiv 2601.16771](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16771); AutoBrep (SIGGRAPH Asia 2025) — [arXiv 2512.03018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.03018); BrepLLM only does classification/captioning and defers B-rep generation to future work — [arXiv 2512.16413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16413)
- Operation-coverage failure is representation-independent but shows in code too: BenchCAD finds models replace sweeps/lofts/twist-extrudes with sketch-extrude; Text2CAD-Bench attributes the L1→L3 IR jump (≈15%→70–90%) to sweep, loft and shell — [arXiv 2605.10865](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10865v1), [arXiv 2605.18430](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18430v1)
- On CADBench, specialized mesh-to-CAD models trained from scratch on sequence/code (CADFit IoU 0.859) far exceed code-generating general VLMs (Claude Opus 4.7 IoU 0.412) under idealized inputs, but specialized models are brittle to modality shift (CADEvolve −0.577 IoU on noisy meshes) — [arXiv 2605.10873](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10873)

### Inferences
- "Code wins" is best supported for frontier general-purpose LLMs (zero/few-shot or lightly tuned), because they transfer Python priors; for small models trained from scratch on reconstruction, the representation matters less than the training distribution and RL on executed geometry (cadrille, CADENA).
- The frontier is not "code vs B-rep" but code with explicit, grounded entity references (pointers, named features, typed ports) — the thing that makes fillets, chamfers and mates reliable, and that both BenchCAD's edit-corruption finding (~64% of edits silently corrupt other features) and CADEngBench-A's entity-grounding failures point at.
- Library choice within "code" (CadQuery vs build123d vs KCL vs FeatureScript) is currently decided by ecosystem/training-data availability, not by evidence; CadQuery is the de facto benchmark standard.

### Gaps
- No controlled study compares CadQuery vs build123d vs OpenSCAD vs FeatureScript for the same LLM and prompts.
- No paper quantifies parametric-JSON vs code for the same backbone beyond Text2CAD-Bench's CadQuery-vs-DeepCAD-sequence comparison.
- B-rep-token LLM generation numbers (BrepARG, ParaCAD) not extracted; these are not LLM-conditioned text-to-CAD systems.

## Key question 2: How good are the best models/agents on parts vs assemblies vs "does it actually work" (physics/assembly reasoning)? Numbers.

### Takeaway
Single parts on synthetic sketch-extrude data are essentially solved (IoU 0.92–0.99, IR ≈0 for CADENA/CADFather/cadrille), but frontier LLMs zero-shot on industrial parts reach only IoU ≈0.27–0.41 and 13–58% valid solids; engineering validity collapses further — 58.1% of executable CADEngBench programs violate a DFM/engineering requirement, only ≈11.5% of assembly-joint requests pass end-to-end kinematics, CAD-bench functional tasks are "near zero" for standalone models, and FEA-checked briefs had zero strict passes in 400 first attempts.

- Text2CAD-Bench limits scope to single solids: "multi-part assemblies with mate constraints out of scope" — [arXiv 2605.18430](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18430v1)

### Inferences
- Rough ladder as of Oct 2026 (best available system per rung): synthetic single-part reconstruction ≈0.98 IoU (trained agents); industrial single-part from image, frontier LLM zero-shot ≈0.3–0.4 IoU, fine-tuned small VLM ≈0.75; executable+engineering-valid single part ≈30% (L1) / FEA-matching ≈35–46%; pairwise joint specification ≈12–16%; functional mechanisms "near zero" without a harness; multi-part briefs with FEA checks ≈0% first-try, ≈20% strict after 10 attempts.
- Structured intermediate specifications (AssemCAD assembly spec, ArtiCAD connectors, ReliCAD specs, FEA agents' blueprint.yaml) consistently add 20–80 points on assembly-structure metrics, indicating the bottleneck is spatial/relational reasoning, not code syntax.
- Numbers are "vendor-era" sensitive: GPT-5.2/Claude 4.5 (CADEngBench, Aug 2026) vs GPT-5.5/Opus 4.7 (FEA agents, May 2026) vs Claude Opus 5.5/GPT-6 Astra (CADFather's BenchCAD table, Oct 2026) — rankings shift month to month.

### Gaps
- No benchmark yet evaluates full multi-body assemblies with kinematic loops (CADEngBench uses pairs; ArtiCAD excludes closed chains).
- CAD-bench per-model and per-harness scores unavailable (empty leaderboard, OpenReview blocked).
- CADMate, COSMO-Agent, Zero-to-CAD, UniCAD, Embodied CAD report no numbers on their abstract pages.
- No published numbers for Zoo Zookeeper, Onshape, Autodesk, Adam, Leo AI on any public benchmark except CAD Arena's 20-prompt validity test.

## Key question 3: Which verification loops are proven effective (execution feedback, geometric metrics, VLM judges, rule checkers, simulation)?

### Takeaway
Layered deterministic verification is the consistent winner: execution/compile feedback alone fixes syntax but not geometry; programmatic kernel checks (bbox, volume, solid validity, IoU to target) and RL on executed-geometry rewards give the biggest gains; VLM visual judges help on structure but "cannot resolve dimensional errors" and plateau after ~2 rounds; FEA/physics feedback roughly doubles-to-triples requirement satisfaction but only when the feedback is concrete (margins, failing selectors); verifier-free consensus over samples is a cheap alternative.

### Cited Findings

**Execution + geometric reward / RL**
- cadrille: RL with reward 10·IoU − 10·invalid drops IR to 0.0 (DeepCAD/Fusion360) and 0.1–0.2 (CC3D) from 1.5–7.7% after SFT; online Dr. CPPO > offline DPO; single-sample RL beats CAD-Recode with 10 samples on IR — [arXiv 2505.22914](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22914v3)
- CAD-Coder (Beihang): GRPO with CD reward: mean CD 74.55 (SFT) → 17.34 (GRPO no CoT) → 6.54 (full); CD-only reward produced invalid code after ~200 steps — needs format/auxiliary rewards — [arXiv 2505.19713](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.19713v3)
- CADENA: stepwise execution with per-step IoU scoring against the target and RL against executed geometry "improves accuracy and lowers the invalid rate" (MCB IoU 72.9→88.2 vs CADEvolve) — [arXiv 2608.00799](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00799)
- BenchCAD: RL on Qwen3-VL-2B gives 0.764 IID / 0.821 OOD vs SFT 0.606–0.623; operation macro recall 17.5%→84.0% — [arXiv 2605.10865](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10865v1)
- CADEvolve data generation: every candidate "must compile to a solid and pass geometry checks before it becomes a parent" — [arXiv 2602.16317](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16317)

**Programmatic geometric validation in agent loops**
- CADSmith: inner execution-error loop + outer OpenCASCADE checks (bbox, volume, solid validity) + independent VLM judge: exec 95%→100%, median IoU 0.8085→0.9629, mean CD 28.37→0.74; motivation: "visual feedback cannot resolve dimensional errors" — [arXiv 2603.26512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26512)
- AssemCAD: five validation gates (phantom bore, disjoint compound, all-coincident parts, port-frame surprise, semantic mis-selection); lesson "Ports should be verified against B-Rep geometry, not just declared" — [arXiv 2607.05123](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05123)
- ArtiCAD: deterministic assembly (no LLM) + cross-stage rollback classifying DESIGN vs CODE errors; removing rollback drops success 100%→95.0%; experience store cuts iterations 4.4→3.1 — [arXiv 2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.10992v1)
- ReliCAD: runtime evidence + verification-feedback loop → 99.8% validity, IoU 0.8753 — [arXiv 2609.22325](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22325)
- CADFather: assistant keeps alternative candidates per part and preserves best valid result; algorithmic proposals are the most important component (IR 0→0.12 on MCB when removed) — [arXiv 2610.09127](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09127)
- AgentsCAD: "geometric grounding via MCP tools is not an optional enhancement but a prerequisite for reliable DFM reasoning" — [arXiv 2607.02448](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02448v2)

**VLM/visual judges and visual Q&A**
- CADCodeVerify (ICLR 2025): VLM generates and answers validation questions on renders; GPT-4 few-shot PC distance 0.155→0.127 (3D-Premise 0.137), compile 96.0→96.5%; Q&A answer accuracy only 64.6–68.2%; gains plateau after 2 iterations; human feedback still better (0.120); "Point Cloud and Hausdorff distances are noisy and don't capture structural or logical flaws" — [arXiv 2410.05340](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05340v2)
- Query2CAD: BLIP2 visual feedback refinement +23.1% success; most gain in first iteration — [arXiv 2406.00144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00144)
- DFM-Redesign: removing the visual review loop roughly triples mean CD (3.496→11.495); but visual review also wrongly rejected a correct chamfer and accepted a mis-oriented base cylinder; orchestration mattered more than Gemini version — [arXiv 2609.05559](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05559)
- FEA agents: 21-view "rich-view" feedback raised GPT-5.5 xhigh mean requirement pass 19.4%→29.3%; but "More views are not uniformly better" (7 views sometimes matched/beat 21); hidden properties (areal density) are invisible to renders — [arXiv 2605.17448](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17448v2)
- BlenderLLM: GPT-4o judge agreed with manual checks 89.7%; LLM–human κ 0.791 vs human–human 0.883; cascade filter precision 81.8% — [arXiv 2412.14203](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.14203)
- ArtiCAD 3-VLM judging: Krippendorff α 0.58–0.64, ICC(2,3) > 0.81 — [arXiv 2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.10992v1)
- Text2CAD-Bench L4 uses GLM-4.6V judge on 8 renders, human spot-check consistent — [arXiv 2605.18430](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18430v1)

**Physics / FEA in the loop**
- Physics-in-the-Loop: physics feedback raised designs in target SF range 22.2%→59.0% (Fisher p=0.0008); physics on/off average iterations 6.0/10.4 (Opus), 4.5/13.0 (Sonnet), 3.3/6.3 (Gemini Flash); removing Planner significantly increased iterations (p=0.0071); "More iterations made results less stable, so stopping criteria are future work" — [arXiv 2605.19717](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19717)
- FEA agents: one CalculiX retry changed mean requirement pass by −1.3 to +30.9 points (avg +13.4); biggest jumps after adding rich-view review (attempt 2) and detailed FEA margins (after attempt 6); "Repeated feedback helps most when it becomes more concrete"; feedback repairs included checker-contract fixes (missing metric aliases), not only engineering fixes — [arXiv 2605.17448](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17448v2)
- CADEngBench: FEA is a verifier not a fixer here — 573/1,809 attempts fail at meshing/BC stage; testing a single parameter state hides failures ("parameter families should be evaluated"; 22/435 verdicts changed away from default) — [arXiv 2608.09296](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09296)
- COSMO-Agent: CAE solve + result parsing + revision as an RL environment with multi-constraint reward (feasibility, toolchain robustness, output validity) — [arXiv 2605.20190](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20190)
- CAD-bench uses Blender rigid-body simulation and thread-profile analysis as functional checks — [ICML 2026](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/67851)

**Verifier-free / selection**
- Consensus selection: picking the sampled program most agreeing with the pool beats random by 1–10% CD across all LLM/prompt variants and beats the SOTA method's verifier on 3 geometric metrics; "the candidate pool itself carries enough signal" — [arXiv 2608.09706](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09706)

**Rule/DFM checkers**
- DFM-Redesign: one verified edit at a time with explicit CNC rules; single-shot redesign "unreliable" — [arXiv 2609.05559](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05559)
- AgentsCAD: deterministic 45° overhang detector + face-adjacency graph before LLM reasoning — [arXiv 2607.02448](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02448v2)
- CADEngBench L1 = scripted engineering/DFM requirement checks; CAD-bench = dimensional/pose gates and thread-profile analysis — [arXiv 2608.09296](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09296), [GitHub cad-bench](https://github.com/cad-bench/cad-bench)

### Inferences
- Ordering of evidence strength: (1) executed-geometry rewards in RL (largest, most replicated: cadrille, CAD-Coder, CADENA, BenchCAD RL); (2) kernel-level programmatic checks in agent loops (CADSmith, AssemCAD, ArtiCAD); (3) FEA feedback (large but noisy gains, costly); (4) VLM judges (useful for gross structure/topology, unreliable for dimensions, plateau at 2 rounds); (5) compile-only feedback (necessary, insufficient).
- Several papers independently converge on "one verified change at a time" (DFM-Redesign, CADENA stepwise, ArtiCAD rollback) and on separating semantic planning from deterministic geometric execution (AssemCAD, ArtiCAD, Physics-in-the-Loop Planner).
- Verification design must guard against benchmark-gaming: FEA agents found repairs that were "checker-contract" fixes, and CADEngBench warns executability and even "reaching FEA" overstate correctness.

### Gaps
- No study isolates the marginal value of a VLM judge on top of programmatic kernel checks with statistical tests (CADSmith has ablations but no significance; DFM-Redesign single runs).
- No published results of LLM agents using commercial constraint solvers/mates (Onshape, Fusion, NX) as verifiers; all academic loops use OpenCASCADE/FreeCAD/Blender/CalculiX/torch-fem.
- Stopping criteria and cost-optimal iteration budgets are explicitly open (Physics-in-the-Loop, FEA agents 68 min/item).

## Key question 4: What is known about human-AI collaboration in engineering design (studies, UX findings)?

### Takeaway
Rigorous user studies of LLM-CAD collaboration are scarce and small (n=2 to n=24; Text2CAD n=5 designers); the available evidence favors "do it with me" guided copilots over full automation for control/learnability, shows humans still out-verify VLM feedback (CADCodeVerify), identifies latency and trust as the main adoption blockers, and reports measured time savings only in adjacent domains (≈20% in a Figma Make RCT), not yet in mechanical CAD.

### Cited Findings
- "Do It For Me vs. Do It With Me" (Khurana, Su, Wang, Chilana; CHI 2025): N=20 comparing fully automated AutoCopilot vs GuidedCopilot (automates trivial steps, step-by-step visual guidance) in feature-rich software for data analysis and visual design; GuidedCopilot outperformed on user control, software utility and learnability, especially for exploratory/creative tasks; AutoCopilot saved time only on simpler visual tasks; follow-up N=10 added in-context preview clips and adaptive instructions — [arXiv 2504.15549](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.15549)
- CADCodeVerify: human-in-the-loop feedback beat the automated VLM feedback (PC distance 0.120 vs 0.137 on a 50-example subset), "so some gap to expert feedback remains" — [arXiv 2410.05340](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05340v2)
- Query2CAD adds human-in-the-loop feedback specifically "to handle false negatives" of the BLIP2 refiner — [arXiv 2406.00144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00144)
- Text2CAD ran a user study with five CAD designers alongside GPT-4V evaluation (preference for Text2CAD 63.24% at expert level) — [arXiv 2409.17106](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.17106)
- Aerospace visual-programming copilot (Yong, Marsh, Silva, Sóbester, Middleton; 2026): Grasshopper + Wingbuilder plugin, GPT-5.4 ReAct; AVPD dataset of 18 expert tasks; trial with two engineers at "a large aircraft manufacturing company": suggestions helpful, slow ReAct inference limited usefulness (best for complex, time-consuming tasks), both willing to use in future; "no publicly announced LLM-based geometric design copilots were in commercial use at aerospace OEMs"; industry prioritizes safety and explainability — [arXiv 2606.16806](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16806)
- Process-modeling copilot mixed-methods study with domain experts (2026): usability acceptable (CUQ 67.2/100) but trust notably lower (48.8%), reliability the top concern; experts struggled to write prompts that reliably produced useful output and the system did not ask clarifying questions — [arXiv 2603.12895](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12895v1)
- Figma Make RCT (Stewart, Anise, Hogan, Griffin; Sep 2026): 50 product designers + 50 product managers, 3 tasks; access to prompt-to-design tool associated with ≈20% shorter completion among completers; gains larger for PMs; "Benefits for professional designers may depend on the task" — [arXiv 2609.26725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26725)
- CADialogue (Zhou, Camba, Company; Computer-Aided Design vol. 191, 2026): multimodal LLM conversational assistant for parametric CAD handling text, selection and image inputs for creation/editing; reported to reduce modeling time and effort and intended "to complement GUI workflows, not replace them" (system evaluation per snippet; full text not retrieved) — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010448525001678)
- Prompt2CAD (2026): conversational CAD with visual feedback on renders; 90.48% success on 63 tasks, 77.78% first attempt (benchmark-style, not a user study) — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050926003224)
- From Idea to CAD (Ocker, Menzel, Sadik, Rios; 2025): VLM multi-agent (requirements engineering, CAD engineering, vision QA) with user refining the model in "an iterative validation loop"; evaluation is demonstrations/ablations, no user study — [arXiv 2503.04417](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04417)
- CADDesigner frames conceptual CAD as an interactive dialogue with requirement analysis and a case knowledge base — [arXiv 2508.01031](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01031)
- Human-LLM collaboration in generative design for customization (J. Manufacturing Systems 2025): three collaboration schemes and a framework for choosing LLM support strategies, vehicle-interior case study; conceptual/case-based, not controlled — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0278612525000731)
- Co-Designer (ACM, Dec 2025): human-AI framework deriving product concepts from user-generated content; 3-stage user study (calibration, collaborative task, surveys on creativity support, usability, trust) — concept ideation rather than CAD; findings not in snippet — [ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3743093.3771654)
- Earlier HCI baselines: Code Copilot 24-person within-subjects study found no reliable completion-time/success gains yet most preferred to keep using it — [CHI 2022 LBW](https://tianyi-zhang.github.io/files/chi2022-lbw-copilot.pdf); "Interactive Programming for Parametric CAD" (Mathur, Pirron, Zufferey 2020) user study: significant improvements over programming-only interface — [EG DigLib](https://diglib.eg.org/items/dc8ef421-03d5-4b1d-a56e-a79cbd56c5e8/full)
- Physics-in-the-Loop designs agents so humans/agents "can read results but can't change them" (deterministic physics/geometry checks) — [arXiv 2605.19717](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19717)
- FEA-agent authors list over-trust in generated CAD and propagation of mechanically invalid load paths as main risks; "Generated CAD is not a certified design tool and should not be used for safety-critical designs without independent engineering review" — [arXiv 2605.17448](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17448v2)
- Zoo's product thesis: "CAD is intent-based, so an AI system must keep design intent coherent and preserve it across follow-up edits" — [zoo.dev](https://zoo.dev/research/zookeeper)
- Vendor claims (Leo AI: 34% fewer design errors, 5–7 h/week saved, citing an unnamed "Nature study") could not be verified — [getleo.ai blog](https://www.getleo.ai/blog/ai-driven-cad-copilot-mechanical-engineering)

### Inferences
- The design pattern emerging from both HCI and agent papers is: deterministic tools own geometry/physics truth, the LLM proposes and explains, the human approves one verified step at a time — matching the CHI finding that guided copilots beat full automation on control and learning.
- Trust/reliability (not capability demos) is the blocker in engineering contexts; the quantitative "does it work" benchmarks (KQ2) explain why.

### Gaps
- No controlled study measuring professional mechanical engineers' productivity or error rates with an LLM CAD copilot (the aerospace trial had n=2; CADialogue full text not retrieved; "Generative AI meets CAD" IJAMT 2025 paywalled; "Fostering empathy in AI-driven co-creation of CAD models" Procedia CIRP 2025 not retrieved).
- No published UX study on Onshape AI Advisor, Zookeeper, Adam or Fusion AI features.
