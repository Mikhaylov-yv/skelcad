# Commercial AI assistants and agent integrations in mechanical CAD — what ships and how it is adopted (as of 2026-10-12)

Research method note: ~30 web searches and ~70 page fetches. Reddit is blocked for the crawler; forum.onshape.com requires SSO; many vendor pages (autodesk.com, ptc.com, siemens blogs, apriori.com, ntop.com, businesswire) return HTTP 403, and engineering.com pages mostly timed out, so several items rely on search-result snippets of those pages (marked "[snippet only]") or on secondary coverage. Competitor-authored sources (Leo AI, TexoCAD, CoLab, MecAgent) are marked as such. No Reddit/eng-tips thread could be read directly — this is a significant gap for "forum complaints".

## Key Question 0 (Objective): What do CAD vendors and startups actually ship, product by product (function, runtime, control, price, reported experience)?

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 the incumbents (Dassault, Autodesk, Siemens, PTC) all ship documentation-grounded chat assistants inside CAD; model-aware or geometry-generating agents exist only as beta/Labs features (SOLIDWORKS LEO via 3DEXPERIENCE Labs tab, Creo beta model-reading, Onshape FeatureScript MCP, Fusion AutoTimeline/AutoAssemble announced). Startups (Zoo, Adam, Backflip, Leo AI, MecAgent) ship generation/conversion of single parts; independent hands-on tests consistently show success on simple parts and failure on multi-feature parts and assemblies. Quantified productivity evidence is almost entirely vendor-reported.
